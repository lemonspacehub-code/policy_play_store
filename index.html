1. Scope and responsible publisher

LemonSpace is responsible for the first-party processing described in this policy. Because individual Services have different features, a permission or data category described here applies only when the Service actually offers the related feature and you choose to use it.

A Service may provide an additional notice at the point where a feature or permission is used. That notice supplements this portfolio policy.

2. Information LemonSpace collects

LemonSpace does not currently collect or store personal data on a LemonSpace backend. The Services do not currently upload account profiles, precise location, photos, videos, faces, biometric templates, contacts, health information, or private feature content to LemonSpace servers.

If this changes, we will update this policy and provide any notice or consent required before the new processing begins.

3. Information processed on your device

A Service may request access to device information or content solely to perform a feature you request. Depending on the Service, this can include app usage, installed-app information, notifications, files, photos, camera input, microphone input, location, or other device capabilities.

Unless a Service clearly states otherwise before processing, this feature data is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to or stored by LemonSpace. You can stop the processing by not using the feature, revoking its Android permission, clearing App data, or uninstalling the Service.

4. Advertising, analytics, and technical information

Some Services use third-party SDKs to display advertising, measure use, deliver configuration, diagnose reliability, prevent fraud, or process purchases. Depending on the Service, region, device settings, age treatment, and your privacy choices, those providers may process information such as:

  • Device model, operating system, language, time zone, country or general region, app version, and network information.
  • IP address, advertising or app-instance identifiers, and approximate location inferred from network information where permitted.
  • App interactions, session events, ad impressions, clicks, diagnostics, crashes, performance measurements, and fraud-prevention signals.
  • Product identifiers, purchase status, subscription status, and transaction tokens supplied by an app store. LemonSpace does not receive full card details.

Adding a materially different provider or purpose may require us to update this policy or show an additional in-app notice. Provider processing is governed by the provider's terms and privacy documentation.

7. Sharing, sale, and targeted advertising

LemonSpace does not sell personal data for money and does not share locally processed feature content with data brokers. Technical information may be processed by the third-party providers needed by a Service, by authorities when legally required, or during a business reorganization with appropriate safeguards.

Some laws define disclosures for personalized or cross-context behavioral advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" even when no money is exchanged. Where required, a Service provides Privacy choices or another mechanism to opt out of covered processing.

8. Storage and retention

On-device information remains until you delete it through the Service, clear the Service's storage, revoke a permission where relevant, or uninstall it. Third-party providers retain technical, advertising, analytics, and transaction information according to their own policies, legal duties, and your settings. We retain emails you send only as long as reasonably needed to respond, protect the Services, or comply with law.

9. International processing

Third-party infrastructure may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, providers use recognized transfer mechanisms and safeguards. Mandatory privacy and consumer rights in your place of residence continue to apply.

11. Children and families

LemonSpace does not knowingly request that children submit directly identifying personal information to a LemonSpace backend. Parents or guardians can contact us if they believe a child sent personal information to us. Individual Services must separately implement any age screen, child-safe SDK configuration, parental action, or advertising restrictions required for their declared audience and region.
